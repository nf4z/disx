# Pride badge artwork

All 33 badges are local, self-contained SVGs in `assets/badge-icons/`. Their identifiers and descriptions live in `src/api/util/utility/prideBadges.ts`. Selecting badges does not contact any external service.

Regenerate the artwork offline with `node scripts/pride-badge-art.cjs`. Check the committed files with `node scripts/pride-badge-art.cjs --check` and run the render regressions with `node --test scripts/tests/pride-badge-art.test.cjs`.

The generator records the chosen digital palette and stripe order for every badge. Physical flags and digital renditions can differ in shade; these palettes represent common renditions rather than a universal color standard. The five-stripe lesbian and gay men flags remain separate choices from their seven-stripe variants. Androgyne has vertical stripes. Bisexual uses 40:20:40 stripe heights; demisexual and demiromantic use a black hoist triangle over a white, narrow colored, and gray field.

Progress geometry follows Daniel Quasar's creator reference, including the narrower innermost white triangle. The intersex-inclusive Progress badge follows Valentino Vecchietti's widened chevron: the yellow hoist field contains the entire purple circle. Both are adapted to the badge's 3:2 viewport. The standalone intersex badge uses Morgan Carpenter's original SVG circle proportions and specified `#FFD800` / `#7902AA` colors.
